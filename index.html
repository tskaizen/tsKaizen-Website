Where the jobs go

You already get leads. Some of them leak.

Before you pay for more traffic, plug the three places where people who already found you drop off.

The site loses them on a phone.

If a homeowner can't find your number, your service area and a way to ask for a quote in a few seconds, they hit back and call the next company.

The fix: a fast mobile site with Call and Quote on every screen.

The call goes unanswered.

You're on a roof or under a sink. The caller doesn't leave a voicemail. They call the next listing on Google.

The fix: an AI receptionist that picks up, answers common questions and books the job.

The quote goes quiet.

Estimates go out and nobody follows up. Happy customers never get asked for a review, so the next homeowner never sees them.

The fix: automatic quote follow-ups and review requests after every job.

Questions

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

Website builds are fixed packages with the price on the pricing page. You see your free demo first, then pick between two options. The price is in writing before any work starts and never grows after you approve it.

I already have a website. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most of my work is rebuilds. I keep the photos and copy worth keeping, and your email keeps working the whole time. If the site is fine and the problem is follow-up, I'll tell you that and quote the follow-up instead.

Do you only work with trades?

Mostly home and mobile service businesses in Orlando and Palm Beach County. If you run a local service business somewhere else in Florida, send it over anyway.

How long does it take?

Your demo is ready within 5 business days. Most sites launch 2 to 3 weeks after you pick a plan and send your logo, photos and service list.

Who owns the site?

You do. The domain is in your name, the code lives in an account you own, and you can take it anywhere once it's paid for.
