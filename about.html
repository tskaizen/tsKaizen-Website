The name

Kaizen means small improvements, made continuously.

The loop in the logo is that idea: find what's slowing a business down, build something simple that fixes it, then keep improving it.

My background is operations: making information move without someone carrying it by hand.

Today that work goes into websites, follow-up and AI tools for local service businesses like NextGen Blinds, Wax On Wheels and MyClearDeal.

How I work

Three rules every project follows.

Small on purpose

No account manager and no handoff to a junior after the first email.

Simple over clever

I'd rather ship something that works on Monday than something you need training to use.

Straight answers

If you don't need what you asked for, I'll tell you before you pay for it.
