Questions

Common questions

Are these prices final?

For the work listed, yes. If your project needs something outside a plan, you get a written quote for that part before anything starts. The price never grows after you approve it.

How do payments work?

Builds are half to start and half at launch, before handoff. Care plans and monthly add-ons bill monthly from launch day, or yearly with 2 months free.

Why is a care plan included in every quote?

Because a site that nobody looks after slowly breaks: forms stop sending, hours go out of date, and Google stops trusting it. You can still skip it and host the site yourself.

What does "founding client pricing" mean?

These prices are lower than where they'll land once I have more launches behind me. The next 5 clients lock them in.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. The quote form gives me enough to price most sites. If you'd rather talk, call or text 561-260-3951.

Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, the code lives in an account you own, and hosting is under your email. On the monthly option, it's fully yours once the 12 months are paid.

What counts as an edit?

Changing hours, prices, photos, a service or a paragraph of text. A brand new page or section is an extra page.

I already have a website. Can you rebuild it?

Yes, at the same prices. I keep the copy and photos worth keeping, and your email keeps working the whole time.
